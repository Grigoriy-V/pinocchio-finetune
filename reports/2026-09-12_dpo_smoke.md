# DPO on two A10s: the smoke, seven starts (2026-09-12)

Item 1 of the queue, the training side: `modal_apps/dpo_app.py` and
`modal_apps/dpo_train.py`, 13 pairs (`data/pairs/v1/dpo_judged.jsonl`
minus three over the 12,288-token ceiling), `gpu="A10:2"`, torchrun with
one process per card, FSDP full shard of the bf16 base, a LoRA of the v1
shape. Each start was `--max-steps 2` on the human's word, each fell on
one thing, each was fixed from the log; the seventh ran.

| # | fell on | fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `DPOConfig` has no `max_prompt_length` / `use_logits_to_keep` (TRL's DPO was rewritten) | fields removed; the trainer read in full |
| 2 | TRL's default `gradient_checkpointing=True` beside FSDP's `activation_checkpointing` | the model's own flag off |
| 3 | OOM in the reference pass, 20 GB held: the policy's activations were still alive | reference pass first, growable CUDA segments |
| 4 | OOM in the backward, 21 GB: both rows of a pair in one graph | each row forward and backward alone, the loss's derivative as its weight |
| 5 | bf16 gradient into an fp32 leaf | the adapter in bf16 (`autocast_adapter_dtype=False`) — necessary, not sufficient |
| 6 | the same | `bf16=False`: accelerate upcasts every trainable parameter to fp32 under FSDP mixed precision |
| 7 | — | 2 steps, adapter and two checkpoints on the Volume |

Two things in that table are the design, not a fix. TRL's trainer asks the
model for the logits of every position; Gemma's vocabulary is 262k, so a
7k-token pair is ~7 GB per sequence in bf16, several times over, beside a
12 GB shard. `LoopDPOTrainer` passes `logits_to_keep`, a tensor of the
positions that predict a completion token (50–160 per pair), and computes
the sigmoid DPO loss on those. And the pair's two rows are not one batch:
both go through the reference and the policy without gradients, which
gives the margin and the derivative of the loss with respect to each
row's log-probability (±β·σ(−margin)); then each row alone goes forward
with gradients and backward with that derivative as its weight. The
gradient is the DPO loss's; only one row's activations exist at a time.
