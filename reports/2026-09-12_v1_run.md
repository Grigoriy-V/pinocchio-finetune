the loss of the first, 0.94–0.96 of target tokens predicted — which is
what training on 34k tokens twice is expected to do, and says nothing
yet about whether the model got better at the harness's work. That is
the held-out measurement, still to come.

(fixed, `7523931`); the GPU choice, since a faster card did not help
